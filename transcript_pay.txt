[00:00 – 00:20] Opening — The Problem
"Every day, financial institutions move trillions of dollars across dozens of payment rails — SWIFT, ACH, RTGS, SEPA. Each has different costs, speeds, and risk profiles. Today, treasury teams rely on spreadsheets and static rule books to route payments. That's slow, inconsistent, and doesn't scale. On a single transaction, choosing the wrong rail can waste thousands of dollars."

[01:00 – 01:40] Platform Overview & Rails
"Our console displays available payment rails for each region — showing speed score, cost efficiency, reliability, processing time, and per-transaction cost. In the US, we support ACH, Wire Transfer, Real-Time Payments, SWIFT International, and Remittance. Each region has its own rail configuration. For example, the UK includes Faster Payments, BACS, and CHAPS. South Africa has PayShap Instant for real-time domestic payments. The platform scores these dynamically against the specific payment — amount, currency, corridor, deadline."

[01:40 – 02:30] Live Orchestration Workflow
"Let's walk through a real payment. We set up a fifty-thousand-dollar transaction from account ACC-001 to ACC-002 on the US to UK corridor. When we hit Orchestrate Payment, the agent workflow activates with four steps. First, Analyze — validates the request and checks all constraints. Second, Score Rails — fetches eligible rails and filters ineligible ones. Third, Select — picks the highest composite score. Fourth, Execute — submits the payment and returns a transaction ID. The entire workflow completes in under five hundred milliseconds. ACH wins at 80.9 — cost-optimized for this amount and corridor. By choosing ACH, we save fourteen dollars and fifty cents compared to Wire Transfer, with full audit trail logging every decision step."
---

[02:30 – 03:20] Agent Intelligence & Technology
"Under the hood, this is a LangGraph StateGraph — a directed acyclic graph of AI agents, each with a specific role. The scoring formula weights three dimensions — cost at thirty percent, speed at forty percent, and reliability at thirty percent. That weighting reflects industry priorities — speed matters most, but cost and reliability balance it. Every agent step emits structured logs with session ID and contextual data, feeding directly into Azure Monitor for production observability. The system is built on FastAPI and Python, running on Azure Container Apps with zero-infrastructure management and auto-scaling. Images are stored in Azure Container Registry, deployments are revision-based with zero-downtime capability."
